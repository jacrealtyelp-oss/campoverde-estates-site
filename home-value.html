Why online estimates miss

Zillow says it plainly: the Zestimate is a starting point, not an appraisal. Here's what an algorithm can't see.

Your upgrades

A new kitchen, a new roof or refrigerated air can add real value. Public records usually don't show them.

Your home's condition

Two homes on the same street can sell far apart because one was kept up and one wasn't.

Your exact location

Backing onto a busy road, a park view, a corner lot or a bigger yard all change what buyers will pay.

This month's market

What's selling now, how fast, and what you'd be competing against. That moves week to week.
