Privacy and cookies

Last updated November 3, 2026

This website is run by John Campoverde, a REALTOR® sponsored by ClearView Realty LLC. This page explains what information the site collects and how it is used.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies. There is no advertising tracking, no analytics tracking and no third-party marketing pixels.

To know how people find me, the site saves a small note in your own browser (local storage) with the first page you visited, the date, the website that sent you, and any campaign tags in the link. If you send a form, that note is included with it. It is never sold or shared with advertisers, and you can clear it any time by clearing your browser's site data. If that ever changes, this page will be updated and you will be asked for your consent before any non-essential cookies are used.

Fonts on this site load from Google Fonts. When your browser downloads them, Google receives standard technical information such as your IP address. See Google's privacy policy.

Information you give me

When you fill out a form on this site, I collect what you enter: for example your name, phone number, email (optional), your goal, your preferred day and time, your timeline, a property address, your budget range, the areas you're interested in, whether you're pre-approved, and any message. Please don't send Social Security numbers, bank details or loan documents through this site. The form is processed and stored by my website host, Netlify.

How I use it

  • To contact you about the appointment you requested and about buying or selling a home.
  • To keep a record of our conversations in my brokerage's customer relationship system.

I do not sell your information. I do not share it with anyone outside my brokerage except when you ask me to (for example, to connect you with a lender) or when the law requires it.

Calls, texts and emails

When you send a form, you're asking me to contact you about that request. I'll reply by call, text or email, whichever you prefer.

Separately, each form has two optional boxes: one to get occasional texts with new listings and market updates, and one to get occasional market-update emails. They're unchecked by default, and checking them is never a condition of working with me. Message and data rates may apply to texts. Reply STOP to any text, use the unsubscribe link in any email, or just tell me, and I'll stop.

Your choices

You can ask me to see, correct or delete the information you sent through this site at any time. Call or text (915) 525-5251 or email jac.realty.elp@gmail.com.
