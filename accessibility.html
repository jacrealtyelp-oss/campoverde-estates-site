Accessibility

Last updated October 27, 2026

I want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification or other assistive technology.

What this site does

  • Aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.
  • Text and buttons meet WCAG color contrast requirements.
  • Every page can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Images and charts have text descriptions for screen readers.
  • Every form field has a label.
  • Animations turn off automatically if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Pages work when zoomed to 200% and on phones.
