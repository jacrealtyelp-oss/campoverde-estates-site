What to have ready for pre-approval

  • Photo ID
  • Your last 30 days of pay stubs
  • W-2s from the last two years (tax returns if you're self-employed)
  • Your last two months of bank statements
  • For VA loans: your Certificate of Eligibility, or the lender can request it

Questions to ask every lender

  • What loan programs do I qualify for, and what's the minimum down payment for each?
  • Do you work with Texas down payment assistance programs?
  • What are your lender fees, and are there points in this rate?
  • How long will my pre-approval last, and how fast can you close?
