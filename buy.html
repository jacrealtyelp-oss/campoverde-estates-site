How buying works with me

From "just looking" to keys in hand. Five steps, no surprises.

  1. Consultation

    We cover your budget, timeline and must-haves, and build your plan.

  2. Pre-approval

    I connect you with a lender so you know your real number before you shop. You're free to choose any lender.

  3. Tour homes

    I set up showings, pull the comps and tell you honestly what each home is worth.

  4. Offer and negotiation

    We write a strong offer, and I negotiate terms on your behalf.

  5. Closing

    I keep inspection, appraisal and every deadline on track until you get the keys.

Before you start

Know your number

A pre-approval shows sellers you're serious and tells you what you can actually afford. Compare four local lenders.

FHA, VA and conventional

I work with buyers using all three. Your lender will tell you which loan fits you best.

Buyer agreement

Before we tour homes, I'll ask you to sign a written buyer representation agreement, which industry rules now require. I'll walk you through every line before you sign.

I'm a real estate agent, not a lender or financial advisor. For loan questions, talk to a licensed loan officer.
