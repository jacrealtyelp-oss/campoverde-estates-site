How I help from a distance

Buying in a city you don't know yet is hard. Here's how we'll handle it.

  1. Start with a call

    By phone or video, we go over your budget, your timeline and what matters to you in a home and a commute.

  2. Get pre-approved early

    So you know your real number before you plan a house-hunting trip. You're free to choose any lender, including VA lenders.

  3. Narrow it down

    I send listings that fit and give you my honest take on each one, so your trip is spent on homes worth seeing.

  4. Make the trip count

    I line up showings for the days you're in town, so you can see as much as possible in a short visit.

  5. Close, even from afar

    I keep inspection, appraisal and every deadline on track and keep you updated the whole way.
